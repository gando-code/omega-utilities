# Semantic Gravity Compactor (SGC)

## Theorem
Bekenstein bound (Bekenstein, 1973): the maximum entropy (information) in a finite region of space is S ≤ 2π·E·R / (ℏ·c·ln 2). The holographic principle (Susskind, 1994; 't Hooft, 1993) further bounds the information in a volume by the area of its boundary: S ≤ A / (4·l_P²).

## Construction (Civilian Tier)
Data is encoded into a representation that approaches the holographic entropy bound. Semantic redundancy is collapsed by mapping the dataset's Kolmogorov structure onto a minimal-area boundary encoding. Civilian implementations achieve large compression ratios on structured data without lossy approximation.

## Civilian Use Cases
- **Archival compression:** 1000×+ compression on structured archives approaching the information-theoretic limit.
- **Long-term storage:** Encode civilizations of records into minimal physical footprints.
- **Bandwidth-constrained transfer:** Maximal information per photon/joule.

## Limitations
Civilian tier approaches the bound asymptotically on structured data. Classified and omega tiers (restricted repo) achieve the bound on arbitrary data and encode information into Planck-scale structures.
